Research activity described in the CV; no separate publication is claimed for this framework.

DFT ACCURACY

Stretched bonds and reaction barriers

Analysis of how self-interaction errors affect transition states and reaction barriers, including extensions beyond the local spin-density approximation.

MANY-BODY METHODS

Development and evaluation of diagonal G and subspace W approximations for bulk semiconductors, with the goal of retaining quasiparticle accuracy while reducing computational demands.
