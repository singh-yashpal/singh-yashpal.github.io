Methods in practice

Research Activities & Projects

Connecting theoretical development, computational workflows, and collaborative materials research.

METHOD DEVELOPMENT

Periodic FLOSIC and Wannier-first methods

Development of a localized-orbital electronic-structure framework for extended materials, including directions toward chiral, helical, and low-symmetry systems.

Research activity described in the CV; no separate publication is claimed for this framework.

DFT ACCURACY

Stretched bonds and reaction barriers

Analysis of how self-interaction errors affect transition states and reaction-barrier energies, including extensions beyond the local spin-density approximation.

Representative paper
MANY-BODY METHODS

Efficient self-consistent GW

Development and evaluation of diagonal G and subspace W approximations for bulk semiconducting systems, retaining quasi-particle accuracy while reducing computational demand.

CATALYSIS

Electrocatalysts and selective hydrogenation

First-principles studies on transition-metal phosphides, carbides, borophene-supported atoms, and doped 2D materials for energy conversion and catalytic selectivity.

OPTOELECTRONICS

Hybrid halides and organic polymers

Modeling electronic states and optical response in halide semiconductors and donor-acceptor conjugated polymers, with links to experimental characterization and materials selection.

LOW-DIMENSIONAL MATERIALS

2D transformations and energy storage

Collaborative computational research on transition-metal dichalcogenides, phosphorenes, and energy storage materials, including stability and transport behavior.
