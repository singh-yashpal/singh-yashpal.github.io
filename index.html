About

A computational and theoretical physicist working at the intersection of electronic-structure theory and materials modeling.

My experience spans density functional theory (DFT), self-interaction correction, GW and coupled-cluster methods, Wannier and localized-orbital approaches, and high-performance scientific computing. I combine method development with materials applications and collaboration across theory, experiment, chemistry, and engineering.

My research connects the accuracy of quantum-mechanical descriptions to practical questions: how reactions proceed, how charge localizes, how materials absorb and emit light, and how simulations can become more efficient.
